About Google Drive backup

Connecting Google is optional. MACPod requests access only to its hidden app-data folder, rather than your other Drive documents. Audio downloads and access credentials are excluded from backups.

MACPod is under active development. Google backup requires developer activation and account authorisation. Backup and recovery do not provide continuous cross-device synchronisation.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for details about local data, external services and backups, and the terms of use. For support, contact the developer through their public GitHub profile. Do not post private feeds, credentials or backup files publicly.
