About Google Drive backup

Connecting Google is optional. MACPod requests access only to its hidden app-data folder, rather than your other Drive documents. Audio downloads and access credentials are excluded from backups.

MACPod is under active development. Connect a Google account in Settings to enable backup; access may be limited to approved accounts while Google authorisation is in testing. Backup and recovery do not provide cross-device synchronisation.

Daily is the default. Continuously backs up metadata changes and saves playback progress on pause, stop, episode switch or every ten minutes while playing. Automatic uploads wait for an unmetered connection and sufficient battery; Android may delay them. Offline changes remain on your device and are combined into the next backup. Manual mode uploads only when you choose Backup now, using your current network.

The Home backup icon opens Backup settings, where you can see the last successful backup, pending work or connection problems. Choose a snapshot from the newest-first list, then preview and confirm its restore. Restore merges saved metadata with your library; existing items remain and newer listening progress wins. Audio can be downloaded again.
