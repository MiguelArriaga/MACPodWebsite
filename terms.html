Backups and development status

MACPod is under active development. Features may change or be unavailable. Cloud snapshots support backup and recovery, rather than continuous multi-device synchronisation. Automatic backup timing depends on Android scheduling, network and battery conditions; operations may be delayed or fail. Continuous backup mode is a series of snapshots, with playback checkpoints, and does not synchronise devices. Keep an independent export of data that matters to you and check that backups and restores work before relying on them.
